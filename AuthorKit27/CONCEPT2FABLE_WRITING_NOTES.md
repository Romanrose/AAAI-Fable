# Concept2Fable AAAI Writing Notes

This file records the writing decisions behind `Concept2Fable_AAAI2027.tex`.
It is not part of the anonymous submission.

## Paper Positioning

Concept2Fable is written as a method paper. The central claim is not that an
LLM can write a fable. The method first recovers a traceable concept mechanism,
then preserves that mechanism through analogical structure mapping, and only
then generates and audits a fable designed to support conceptual understanding.

The evidence boundary is explicit:

- Pilot12 is a completed feasibility experiment: 12 concepts, 3 mapping
  methods, 3 candidates per method and concept, and 108 records in total.
- Core80 and Human32 have frozen manifests but no completed result tables.
- FullKG names the 6,574 available concept targets; it is not a completed story
  dataset.
- No student learning gain is claimed before a controlled human study.

## What We Learn from Three AAAI Papers

### SS-GEN (AAAI 2025)

Writing pattern adopted:

1. Begin with a concrete educational need and explain why the artifact has
   domain-specific constraints.
2. Translate those constraints into the generation method rather than treating
   them as a prompt appendix.
3. Define evaluation criteria for the intended artifact and combine automatic
   evaluation with trained human review.

Concept2Fable follows this pattern by motivating conceptual understanding,
defining mechanism preservation and lexical concealment, and proposing a
Human32 protocol. It does not copy SS-GEN's scale claims or infer educational
effectiveness from LLM ratings.

### Amar (AAAI 2025)

Writing pattern adopted:

1. Identify a specific failure of existing retrieval: more graph context can
   introduce partially relevant evidence and noise.
2. Introduce each retrieval component as a response to that failure.
3. Design ablations that remove one component or evidence aspect at a time,
   followed by a retrieval-budget analysis.

Concept2Fable uses the same problem-to-module rhythm for controlled adaptive
two-hop retrieval. The planned comparison is definition only versus one hop
versus adaptive two hop, with the same downstream mechanism builder.

### StoryBox (AAAI 2026)

Writing pattern adopted:

1. Give every agent a concrete responsibility in the generation process.
2. Present experimental settings, metrics, baseline families, automatic
   evaluation, human evaluation, ablations, and cases as separate units.
3. Compare human and automatic judgments instead of assuming they agree.

Concept2Fable applies this structure to Generator, Aligner, Judge, and Reviser.
It does not borrow StoryBox's open-ended simulation claim because the present
pipeline follows a frozen analogy plan.

## Final Writing Checks

- Lead sections with a failure or research question, then motivate the module.
- Keep `RetrievalPackage` distinct from `Concept Mechanism Graph`.
- Use `Copycat-inspired`; do not claim a complete implementation of classical
  Copycat.
- Keep LLM-Judge acceptance separate from structural exact match and human
  learning outcomes.
- Replace every `TBD` only with a value generated by the frozen evaluation
  protocol and traceable to a manifest.
- Add a positive case only after confirming valid encoding, no leakage, exact
  evidence, and mechanism provenance.
